# #66 A detection head on frozen features

Set out to: train a light person detector on the cached SigLIP 2 features, per camera, fold and label fraction, and report `ap_iou25` beside `ap_iou50`.
Chose: a CenterNet head (centre heatmap, sub-cell offset, log size) on a grid four times finer than the features, made by pixel shuffle. One feature cell is 37 px (thermal) to 183 px (4K RGB), so the whole problem is saying where in the cell the person is. Sizes are measured in cells, so a 4K and a 2K frame of the same scene get the same targets.
Expected before running: RGB poor (`ap_iou25` 0.05 to 0.2), thermal better (0.2 to 0.5), `ap_iou50` far below `ap_iou25` for RGB.
Surprising:
- The completeness check found that the cache is missing 5 RGB and 3 thermal frames that the per-camera fold manifests label: the cache picks frames from `all_pairs.jsonl`, and these frames have no partner in the other camera. Normal runs refuse until they are cached.
- MtErie, the first fold's test site-day, is the only 4K RGB site-day; every training frame for that fold is 1920×1080. All 1,770 of its test people are 32 px or larger, so its "tiny people" result is empty.
- A one-cell target is exactly CenterNet with the Gaussian radius at zero: every person is smaller than a sub-cell.
Scratch check only (branch run, not a result): RGB fold MtErie at 100% ran in about 4 minutes end to end.
Sources: docs/detection-head-design.md; results table to come from runs on `main`.
