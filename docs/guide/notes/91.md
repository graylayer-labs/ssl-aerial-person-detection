# #91 Do small-label heads stop too early?

Set out to: check a side finding from #68, where 120 extra steps lifted the thermal 10% heads from 0.129 to 0.162, suggesting the first bar was under-trained.
Chose: read the #66 training curves before running anything.
Expected before running: validation still rising at the end at 1% to 10%.
Surprising:
- The opposite. Most low-label runs picked step 250, the first evaluation, and validation fell from there: the heads overfit within a few hundred steps on 30 to 700 frames.
- Re-running the 24 low-label cells with an evaluation every 25 steps found peaks at steps 25 to 150, yet moved test means by at most 0.01. A precise peak on a noisy validation curve was worth nothing on held-out sites.
- So the #68 control's gain was a restarted schedule plus the best of two noisy evaluations, not a better head.
Sources: docs/detection-head-results.md "Step selection check"; outputs/head-eval25; issue #91.
